
# Product Success Metrics

## North Star Metric
Weekly Active Opportunity Organisers:
Number of users who save or update the status of at least one opportunity in a week.

## Activation
Percentage of new users who save their first opportunity.

## Engagement
- Opportunities viewed per active user
- Saved opportunities per active user
- Weekly active users

## Retention
Percentage of activated users who return in a later week.

## Task Success
Percentage of test participants who can find and save an opportunity successfully.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Incorrect or expired listing reports
- Search failure rate
- Accessibility issues
- User complaints about misleading listings

## Measurement Plan
Instrument product events such as:
- opportunity_viewed
- search_performed
- filter_applied
- opportunity_saved
- application_status_updated

Collect a baseline before setting numerical targets.

## Important
These are proposed product metrics. No actual user performance results are claimed.
