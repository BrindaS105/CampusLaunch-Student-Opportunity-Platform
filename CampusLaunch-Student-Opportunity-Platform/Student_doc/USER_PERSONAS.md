
# User Personas

These are hypothetical personas for initial product planning. They are not real interview participants.

## Persona 1: Internship Seeker

Profile:
- Undergraduate student
- Looking for software and data internships
- Uses multiple websites to discover openings

Needs:
- Relevant opportunities
- Search and category filters
- Application tracking

Pain Points:
- Difficulty organising saved listings
- Forgetting deadlines

## Persona 2: Scholarship Applicant

Profile:
- College student looking for financial support
- Needs to compare eligibility and deadlines

Needs:
- Scholarship discovery
- Deadline visibility
- Saved list

Pain Points:
- Information spread across different sources
- Unclear application status

## Research Plan

Before treating these personas as validated:
1. Interview 5–8 students.
2. Ask how they currently find opportunities.
3. Ask how they track deadlines.
4. Observe how they use existing tools.
5. Record repeated problems.
6. Update personas based on actual findings.

Do not report planned interviews as completed interviews.
